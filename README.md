# Awesome-Digital-Sales-Room

# Top Digital Sales Room Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Buyer Enablement Portals, Mutual Action Plans, Deal Collaboration Spaces & Content Rooms for Complex Sales*  
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Digital Sales Rooms**. These systems give buyers a branded, shared workspace for proposals, content, next steps, and stakeholder collaboration—replacing email threads with a single deal hub.

**Examples** include GetAccept, DealRoom, Sendtrumpet / Trumpet, Recapped, Dock, Aligned, Journeybee, ClientPoint, and Salesflow (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Dedicated digital sales room products are almost entirely commercial. Open building blocks include **document collaboration**, **CRM portals**, **e-sign**, and **self-hosted file/rooms** stacks that teams assemble into lightweight deal rooms. This section lists every significant relevant approach found.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[GetAccept, Dock, Aligned, Recapped](https://www.getaccept.com/)**  
  Digital sales room and buyer enablement platforms—shared content, mutual action plans, engagement analytics, and deal collaboration.

- **[Trumpet / Sendtrumpet, Journeybee, ClientPoint, Salesflow](https://www.trumpet.app/)**  
  Modern deal rooms and proposal experiences focused on buyer journeys and multi-stakeholder closing.

- **[DealRoom (M&A / deal collaboration)](https://dealroom.net/)**  
  Structured deal collaboration often used in complex B2B and transaction workflows.

- **[Other commercial digital sales room platforms](https://www.getaccept.com/)**  
  Additional content rooms, CPQ-adjacent portals, and revenue enablement spaces.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Nextcloud](https://github.com/nextcloud/server)**  
  Open self-hosted collaboration suite—share links, password-protected folders, Talk, and OnlyOffice/Collabora for buyer-facing deal content rooms.

- **[Documenso](https://github.com/documenso/documenso)**  
  Open-source e-signature platform—useful for proposal and contract steps inside a DIY digital sales room.

- **[Twenty CRM](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty)**  
  Open-source CRM that can be extended with shared deal views and external-facing deal context for buyers.

- **[Outline / BookStack / Wiki.js](https://github.com/outline/outline)**  
  Open knowledge bases used to publish structured deal playbooks, mutual action plans, and buyer resources.

- **[HedgeDoc / Collabora / OnlyOffice](https://github.com/hedgedoc/hedgedoc)**  
  Open collaborative document editing for joint proposal and action-plan authoring with buyers.

- **[Formbricks / open form + feedback tools](https://github.com/formbricks/formbricks)**  
  Open survey and feedback collection for stakeholder discovery inside a deal process.

- **[Custom portal starters (Next.js + auth)](https://github.com/search?q=customer+portal+OR+deal+room+open+source)**  
  Community templates for authenticated buyer portals that host documents, tasks, and status.

- **[Cal.com + open scheduling](https://github.com/calcom/cal.com)**  
  Open scheduling embedded in deal rooms for next-step meetings without email ping-pong.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Content room**: Nextcloud shared folders with branded links and expiry.
- **Contracts**: Documenso for signature workflows.
- **Mutual action plans**: Outline/BookStack pages with checklists.
- **CRM backbone**: Twenty or other open CRM for internal deal state.
- **Composable stacks**: Nextcloud + Documenso + Cal.com + open CRM = lightweight DIY digital sales room.
- Commercial DSR platforms still lead in engagement analytics, buyer UX, and sales-team workflow integration.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  
**Nextcloud** (content) + **Documenso** (sign) + **open wiki** (MAP) + **Cal.com** (meetings) form a practical open deal-room stack.  
Commercial products (GetAccept, Dock, Aligned, Trumpet, Recapped, etc.) provide polished buyer experiences and CRM analytics.  
Startups may assemble open tools; enterprise sales teams usually adopt dedicated DSR software. Fully open digital sales rooms are possible but require more integration work than turnkey SaaS.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Digital sales rooms host commercial content and sometimes personal data of buyers. Secure access, expire links, and respect confidentiality agreements. Engagement tracking should be disclosed where required by law or policy.
- Open-source stacks offer control and cost savings but place security, branding, and maintenance on your team. Commercial platforms shift product and support burden to the vendor. Neither replaces strong sales process and relationship management.
